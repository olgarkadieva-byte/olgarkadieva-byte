## Featured project

### [AI Governance & Measurement Kit](https://github.com/olgarkadieva-byte/ai-governance-measurement-kit)

A free, framework-aligned toolkit for governing AI systems and measuring their impact.

- 34 AI controls, including 8 for AI agents, mapped to the **NIST AI RMF**, **ISO/IEC 42001** and the **EU AI Act**
- A five-dimension risk scoring model and EU AI Act tier screening guide
- An AI tool telemetry spec for measuring adoption, time saved and ROI
- A tested Python package (`aigov`) with CI
